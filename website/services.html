Our Services

Operational support at every stage.

From freshly funded to fully scaled, we bring the right expertise at the right moment—so you never outgrow your infrastructure or lose momentum chasing it.

Strategic Operational Mapping

We work alongside you to build a tailored, scalable roadmap from day one, connecting every dot between big-picture growth and daily execution. Our style is proactive: we identify the challenges you'll face long before you do, create strategies to resolve them, and give you the confidence to make bold decisions at every stage.

G&A Infrastructure Evaluation & Optimization

We visit your site, assess your infrastructure, and make actionable recommendations for more efficient systems, policies, workflows, and processes. Whether you need a quick tune-up or a complete overhaul, we help you spot hidden inefficiencies and create remedies you can implement right away.

Complete G&A Setup

We build, manage, and optimize HR, finance, communications, IT, offices, and specialized facilities—including real estate, space planning, relocation, full move management, and end-to-end vendor evaluation and oversight—so you can stay focused on innovation.

Boots-on-the-Ground Project Implementation

Most consultancies leave you with a plan and a wish for success. We do it all: relocation and buildout, HRIS and performance systems, laboratory compliance, office and lab management, IT and vendor oversight, communications, and more. We plan it with you, then we do it for you.

Office & Infrastructure Management

Just need someone to run your current operation? We take over the day-to-day management of your office or infrastructure, troubleshooting issues, handling logistics, and keeping everything running smoothly. Whether you need full enterprise support or a practical office manager, our team becomes an extension of yours.

Flexible Consulting Models

Need a COO but can't justify the full-time hire? Or expert help on a specific project without a long-term commitment? We provide fractional leadership for ongoing operational support or targeted consulting for critical initiatives. Choose a partnership that fits your need, budget, and timeline.

Services by Stage

The right support for where you are right now.

Every startup is unique. We tailor our engagement to match your current stage, team size, and growth goals.

Stage 1

Newly Funded

Seed to Series A — typically 2–20 people

You just closed your round. The science is solid. Now you need the infrastructure to match. We help you build the right foundation—lean, compliant, and ready to scale—without wasting time or money on the wrong systems.

  • Entity setup and early compliance frameworks
  • HR foundations: offer letters, handbooks, onboarding workflows
  • Benefits selection and HRIS setup
  • Financial systems and early-stage CFO support
  • Lab and office search, negotiation, and buildout coordination
  • Vendor evaluation and first-contract negotiation
Stage 2

Incubator to Independence

Series A to B — typically 20–60 people

You're outgrowing incubator space and informal processes. We manage your transition to independent facilities while professionalizing your operations so nothing falls through the cracks during a critical phase.

  • Facility relocation and full move management
  • Lab build-out: equipment, compliance, EH&S programs
  • Scaled HR: performance management, compensation frameworks
  • IT infrastructure and security policies
  • Culture and communications programs
  • Operational audits and process re-engineering
Stage 3

Ready to Scale

Series B+ — 60+ people, multiple sites

You're growing fast across multiple locations. We provide senior-level operational leadership to harmonize systems, manage complexity, and keep your culture intact as you scale toward clinical milestones or liquidity events.

  • Multi-site operational strategy and coordination
  • Fractional COO / VP Operations support
  • Enterprise HRIS, ERP, and financial system upgrades
  • M&A integration support and due diligence preparation
  • Real estate portfolio management
  • Our self-replacement strategy: we hire you out of our role when you're ready
