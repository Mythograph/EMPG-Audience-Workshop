About Us

We bridge the critical gap between innovation and operational excellence.

At E.M. Perry Group, we exist so early-stage startups can focus entirely on their breakthrough science—while we build the operational infrastructure that makes growth possible.

Why This Matters

The operational gap is real—and costly.

While you're focused on groundbreaking research and securing funding, your company operations can't be overlooked.

Many startup founders don't realize they need comprehensive operational support until it's too late: when inefficiencies, compliance issues, and operational chaos start hindering growth.

From idea to outcomes, we help biotech companies grow fast and strong to serve the people who need them.

60%

of biotech startups fail within the first five years, with operational issues being a key factor.

— Nature Biotechnology

Most founders don't know what they don't know. Operational gaps don't announce themselves—they accumulate quietly until they become crises. We see them coming.

We Understand That

You're not alone in these challenges.

You're innovators, not operations nerds

Your expertise is in science and discovery—not in HR policy, vendor contracts, or IT infrastructure. That's exactly where we come in.

Full C-suite teams aren't always affordable

Early-stage startups can't always justify full-time executive hires across every G&A function—but the work still needs to get done.

Generic VC advice isn't enough

Venture capital recommendations often give one-size-fits-all solutions for highly unique problems. Your company needs tailored operational strategy.

Traditional COOs aren't built for this

Traditional COOs focus more on business development than day-to-day operations. Early-stage startups need someone who gets their hands dirty—every day.

Our Approach

Four principles that shape everything we do.

01

Proactive Planning

We identify challenges you'll face long before you do and create strategies to resolve them. Our approach is birds-eye vision, then ground-level work. We're always three steps ahead so you never get caught off guard.

02

Hands-On Implementation

Most consultancies leave you with recommendations and plans. Once we decide on the best approach, our team integrates with yours—tackling daily work on site for as long as it takes. Strategy means nothing without execution.

03

Vendor Agnostic

Our loyalty is to your results, not any vendor. We challenge industry norms and protect you from unsuitable long-term contracts. Every recommendation we make is in your interest—full stop.

04

Self-Replacement Model

As our clients grow, we help hire and train our own replacements to become your full-time employees. We leave you ready for what's next, not reliant on us. Your long-term independence is our definition of success.

Our Founder

Emily Perry

Founder & Managing Director

Emily Perry is a strategic operations leader specializing in early-stage biotech startups. As founder, Emily leverages her extensive experience building companies from the ground up to provide comprehensive operational support for clients.

Emily created E.M. Perry Group after recognizing a critical need in the startup industry for experienced operational leadership in early-stage companies. Her track record includes successfully scaling biotech startups from 3 to more than 60 employees, managing lab build-outs and relocations, and implementing robust operational infrastructures.

Emily's hands-on approach and ability to connect the dots across business functions enable innovators to focus on their groundbreaking work while her team handles the critical behind-the-scenes details that keep biotech companies running smoothly.

Harvard Business School Blavatnik Fellowship

Emily mentors fellows in the Harvard Business School Blavatnik Fellowship in Life Science Entrepreneurship, a prestigious program supporting HBS alumni and Harvard-affiliated researchers as they commercialize promising life science technologies.
