Remix Therapeutics

From founding team to 60+ employees across three facilities—a complete operational journey spanning six years.

Timeline 2017–2023
Company Stage Seed → Series B+
Team Growth 4 → 60+ employees
Facilities 3 locations

Overview

Remix Therapeutics is an RNA splicing company focused on developing medicines for patients with serious diseases. When E.M. Perry Group joined in the early days, Remix had four employees, no formal operations infrastructure, and a vision to change how RNA-targeted therapies are discovered and developed.

Over six years, we partnered with Remix through every major inflection point—three facility moves, multiple funding rounds, and growth to a team of more than 60 scientists and operators. Our role evolved as the company did: from building the very first systems to eventually stepping back as a world-class internal team took the reins.

The Challenge

Like most early-stage biotechs, Remix's founders were scientists first. They had world-class expertise in RNA biology and drug discovery—but no time, interest, or bandwidth for the operational complexity of building a company from scratch.

The challenges compounded at every stage:

  • No HR, finance, or compliance infrastructure at founding
  • Rapid headcount growth outpacing informal systems and processes
  • Three facility transitions, including a full lab buildout
  • The need to maintain culture and morale through constant change
  • Pressure to have enterprise-grade operations while still acting like a startup

Our Approach

Stage 1 · 2017–2018

Building the Foundation

We started at the beginning: entity setup, benefit programs, and the first HR policies. Every system we built was designed to be lean enough for a four-person team but robust enough to scale to fifty. We set up the financial infrastructure, established vendor relationships, and created onboarding processes that would grow with the company.

  • HR foundation: employee handbook, offer letter templates, onboarding workflows
  • Benefits selection and HRIS implementation
  • Financial systems and early bookkeeping infrastructure
  • Lab and office space search and negotiation at LabCentral, Cambridge
  • First vendor contracts and IT setup
Stage 2 · 2019–2021

From Incubator to Independence

As Remix closed a significant Series A, it was time to leave shared incubator space and establish an independent facility. We managed the full relocation—real estate search, lease negotiation, buildout management, and move coordination—while simultaneously scaling every people and finance system to match the growing team.

  • Led search and selection of independent lab and office space in Cambridge, MA
  • Coordinated full lab buildout: EH&S program, equipment installation, compliance
  • Managed complete office relocation with zero science disruption
  • Scaled HR: performance management, compensation bands, manager training
  • Implemented enterprise HRIS and ATS systems
  • Developed culture and communications programs for a 30+ person team
Stage 3 · 2021–2023

Scaling to Enterprise

With a Series B and growing team of 60+, Remix needed the operational infrastructure of a mature company—and the flexibility of a startup. We led a third facility expansion, built out the enterprise-grade systems, and spent this final phase executing our self-replacement strategy: hiring and developing the internal operations team that would eventually take over.

  • Third facility expansion: additional lab and office space
  • Enterprise-level HR, finance, and IT infrastructure
  • Built and mentored internal operations team
  • Transitioned full operational ownership to internal leadership
  • Real estate portfolio management across multiple sites
