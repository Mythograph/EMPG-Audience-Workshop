Arrakis Therapeutics

Seamless scaling and facility transitions during a critical growth phase—without missing a beat.

Timeline 2022–2023
Stage Series B Growth Phase
Engagement Type Scaling & Facility Transitions
Location Waltham, MA

Overview

Arrakis Therapeutics is a pioneer in RNA-targeted drug discovery, developing small molecule drugs that target disease-causing RNA—a platform with the potential to address a vast range of diseases previously considered undruggable. Having built an impressive scientific platform and secured significant Series B funding, Arrakis was in a critical growth phase requiring serious operational firepower.

E.M. Perry Group was brought in to provide the operational leadership and execution capacity needed to scale the company and manage complex facility transitions—without disrupting the science.

The Challenge

Arrakis was at an inflection point common to many post-Series B biotechs: the science had outpaced the infrastructure. The team was growing rapidly, the existing facility was reaching capacity, and the systems built for a smaller company were straining under the weight of a larger one.

  • Rapid headcount growth straining HR systems and culture
  • Lab and office space at capacity—expansion urgently needed
  • Operational processes that worked at 30 people struggling at 60+
  • Leadership bandwidth consumed by science and fundraising, not operations
  • Risk of culture dilution as the team scaled quickly
  • Complex facility transition requiring meticulous planning to avoid science disruption

Our Approach

We embedded with the Arrakis leadership team as senior operational partners, focusing on three simultaneous tracks: scaling people systems, managing the facility transition, and ensuring culture remained intact through rapid growth.

Track 1 · People & HR

Scaling People Systems for a Growing Team

  • HRIS audit and upgrade to enterprise-grade platform
  • Compensation framework redesign to support competitive hiring
  • Performance management system implementation
  • Manager training and development programs
  • Onboarding process redesign to maintain culture through rapid hiring
  • Employee engagement programs and all-hands cadence
Track 2 · Facilities

Managing Complex Facility Expansion

  • Lab and office space needs assessment and capacity planning
  • Real estate search and broker management in competitive Waltham/Cambridge market
  • Lease negotiation and tenant improvement oversight
  • New facility buildout coordination: lab design, equipment, EH&S compliance
  • Phased relocation plan to minimize science and operations disruption
  • Full move management and go-live support
Track 3 · Operations & Infrastructure

Hardening the Operational Foundation

  • Vendor portfolio audit and renegotiation of key contracts
  • IT infrastructure upgrade for expanded headcount and facilities
  • Finance and accounting process optimization
  • Communications strategy for a growing, distributed team
  • EH&S program expansion and compliance management

Results

Seamless
Facility transition with zero science disruption
100%
On-time delivery of facility expansion milestones
Scalable
HR and people systems built to support continued growth
Intact
Company culture maintained through rapid scaling

Arrakis successfully scaled through one of its most operationally demanding periods—expanding its facilities, growing its team, and advancing its RNA-targeted platform—with full operational continuity. Leadership remained focused on science and clinical strategy while we handled the complexity of building a bigger, better company behind the scenes.
