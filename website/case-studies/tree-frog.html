Tree Frog Therapeutics

Helping a French biotech establish U.S. operations from scratch—with zero compliance violations.

Timeline 2023–2024
Origin Bordeaux, France
Engagement Type U.S. Market Entry
Outcome 0 Compliance Violations

Overview

Tree Frog Therapeutics is a French biotech company pioneering a proprietary cell therapy platform for neurological diseases. Having built a strong scientific and commercial foundation in Europe, Tree Frog made the strategic decision to expand into the United States—a critical step for accessing U.S. capital markets, clinical infrastructure, and talent.

They needed a partner who could navigate U.S. entity formation, regulatory compliance, HR law, and operational setup with precision—while their leadership team stayed focused on science and fundraising in France.

The Challenge

Establishing U.S. operations as a foreign company is far more complex than it appears. Tree Frog faced a matrix of challenges:

  • No existing U.S. legal entity, EIN, or banking relationships
  • Unfamiliarity with U.S. employment law, benefits requirements, and HR norms
  • No local relationships with real estate brokers, vendors, or service providers
  • Leadership team 6 time zones away, requiring asynchronous decision-making
  • Tight timeline to establish credibility with U.S. investors and partners
  • Need for a trusted local operational presence before hiring a full-time U.S. team

Our Approach

We served as Tree Frog's boots-on-the-ground operational partner in the U.S.—the trusted local presence their leadership could rely on to get everything done correctly and quickly.

Phase 1 · Entity & Compliance

Establishing the Legal & Financial Foundation

  • U.S. entity formation: C-corporation setup, EIN, registered agent
  • U.S. banking and financial account establishment
  • Federal and state employer registration and tax enrollment
  • Initial accounting and financial reporting infrastructure
  • Legal counsel coordination for employment agreements and IP protection
Phase 2 · HR & People

Building a Compliant U.S. Employment Framework

  • U.S. employee handbook and HR policy development
  • Benefits design and carrier selection for U.S. employees
  • HRIS setup and payroll provider implementation
  • Onboarding process for first U.S. hires
  • Guidance on U.S. employment law nuances for French leadership
Phase 3 · Operations & Presence

Creating a Credible U.S. Operational Footprint

  • Boston-area office space search and lease negotiation
  • IT setup and communications infrastructure
  • U.S. vendor network establishment (legal, finance, HR, IT)
  • Ongoing operational management until internal hire was ready

Results

Compliance violations across all filings and employment matters
< 90 days
From engagement start to fully operational U.S. presence
100%

Tree Frog established a credible, compliant U.S. operational presence that positioned the company for U.S. fundraising and clinical partnerships—without a single compliance misstep. Their European leadership was able to remain fully focused on science while we handled every U.S. operational detail.
